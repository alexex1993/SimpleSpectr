Bir spektrogramı okumak için ihtiyacınız olan her şey

Profesyonel düzeyde araçlar — ücretsiz, yerel bir Mac uygulamasında.

🎚️

Doğru FFT analizi

Short-time Fourier transform with amplitude normalization, computed via Apple's Accelerate (vDSP), so hover reports true dBFS. Pick the FFT size (256 – 16384), the overlap (up to 87.5 %) and the window — Hann, Blackman-Harris, Kaiser or Flattop.

🎹

5 frekans ölçeği

Linear, Logarithmic (with note names, A4 = 440 Hz), Mel, Bark, and ERB. Each perceptual scale warps the rows so labels and image always agree.

🔈

Kanal modları

Analyze stereo as Mix (mono down-mix), Left, Right, Mid/Side (M/S) sum and difference, Stereo (L/R stacked one above the other), or Correlation that colors a single plot by how L and R relate at each frequency — red anti-phase, blue in phase.

🎙️

Canlı mikrofon girişi

Record from the mic and watch a full-screen spectrogram build in real time. The captured FLAC is reopened through the full analysis path.

📐

Ölçüm bölgesi

Drag a rectangle for instant Δ-time, Δ-frequency, musical interval, and true-amplitude stats: RMS, sample peak dBFS, 4× oversampled true peak dBTP, and BS.1770 LUFS.

📊

Spektrum dilimi

Double-click any moment to pop a 1-D amplitude-vs-frequency plot of that exact STFT column — handy for reading peaks and spectral shape.

🎯

Harmonik imleç

Overlay faint guide lines at the overtone series (2×, 3×, 4×…) above the frequency under the cursor to separate pitched content from noise.

🌊

Dalga formu genel görünümü

A time-domain amplitude lane above the spectrogram, locked to the same time axis and zoom, so you can see dynamics alongside frequency.

🔍

Gezinme çapraz okuması

Move the cursor anywhere to read the exact time, frequency, and signal level (dB) at that point, without re-reading pixels.

⬇️

Veri dışa aktarımı

Export the actual STFT magnitudes as CSV or JSON, save the rendered image as PNG, or dump your markers — with range and format options.

🎬

3D geçiş ve 2D çubuk videosu

Render the spectrogram as a share-ready .mp4 in two styles: a moving 3D flyover landscape synced to the music, or classic 2D bars — segmented bars over a glowing baseline with a mirrored reflection. Pick 16:9, 9:16, or 1:1, up to 4K, tune the camera or the bars, and set your own background and bar colors. Built for Reels, TikTok, Shorts, and YouTube.

🎨

9 algısal renk haritası

Inferno, Viridis, Magma, Plasma, Turbo, Cividis, Jet, Hot, and Grayscale — interpolated in Oklab space, with live previews and an adjustable dB window. Correlation mode uses its own diverging red↔blue map.

🎛️

Seviyeler ve büyüklük

Set the color window in dBFS — floor, ceiling and a reference-level gain that lifts quiet material — and map values as log dB or linear amplitude. Everything re-renders instantly from the cached grid, with no re-analysis.

ℹ️

Dosya bilgileri

One click reports container, codec, lossless or lossy compression, PCM sample format, sample rate and Nyquist, channels, bit depth, bitrate, frames per packet, duration, total frames and file size.

▶️

Oynatma ve senkronizasyon

A bottom player plays the file, a synced playhead moves across the spectrogram, click-and-drag seeks, and the view auto-scrolls to follow playback.

⏱️

İşaretler ve yakınlaştırma

Drop labelled markers on the timeline, stretch the plot horizontally with time zoom, and reopen recent files across launches.

💾

Belleğe hafif

Audio is decoded and analyzed as a stream, never held in full — so even very long files render without running out of memory.

Nasıl çalışır

Audio is decoded with Core Audio and streamed through the analyzer, so long files never sit fully in memory. The channel you picked (mix, left, right, mid/side — or both channels at once for the stereo and correlation modes) is transformed via a short-time Fourier transform (your chosen window + real FFT) using Accelerate's vDSP. The one-sided spectrum is amplitude-normalized and window-corrected, magnitudes are converted to dB, and mapped through a perceptual colormap into a crisp image — low frequencies at the bottom, high at the top, time advancing left to right. The dB grid is cached, so switching palette, scale or dB window re-renders instantly without touching the audio again.
